            Help /

            Friends outside the house

            Let friends who don't live here join the servers you choose. Only players on a server's allowlist can get in, and their Xbox sign-in is always checked. Everything else stays private.

            Outside access

            A hostname from your own domain or a dynamic DNS service (like DuckDNS) keeps working when your internet address changes. With a fixed address, the IP works too.

              Your router

              This needs UPnP turned on in the router. Without it, add the forwards below in the router's settings (often under "Port forwarding" or "Virtual servers"). Never forward port 8443: that's this panel, and it should stay inside the house.

              Friends on Xbox, PlayStation and Switch

              Consoles can't type a server address, so a friend changes their console's DNS to this home's address, joins a featured server such as The Hive, and lands in the list of servers open to them. This forwards ports 19132 and 53 as well. The panel only answers the featured-server names for them; their console gets everything else from its second DNS. It works on most consoles; if one can't get online that way, the invite also explains a public alternative.
