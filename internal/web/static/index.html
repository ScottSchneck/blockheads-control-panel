Your Xbox gamertag goes on the allowlist and becomes an operator on every server you add, so you can always get in.

At home, the easy way: open Minecraft, go to Play → Friends, and under LAN Games pick . Then pick a server. A PS5 finds it this way with no other setup.

If it isn't there (or on an Xbox or Switch): set the console's DNS to , restart the console, open Minecraft, go to Servers and join any featured server, such as The Hive. You'll land in the menu of your servers.

Players still need to be on a server's allowlist; anyone the menu sends in who isn't shows up under Tried to join on that server's Players tab, with an Allow button.
