        Help

        Joining from consoles

        At home, the easy way

        Open Minecraft, go to Play → Friends, and under LAN Games pick . Then pick a server. A PS5 finds it this way with no other setup.

        If it isn't there, or on an Xbox or Switch

        1. Set the console's DNS to .
        2. Restart the console.
        3. Open Minecraft, go to Servers, and join any featured server, such as The Hive.
        4. You land in the menu of your servers. Pick one.

        Letting people in

        Players need to be on a server's allowlist. Anyone the menu sends to a server who isn't shows up under Tried to join, with an Allow button.

        The panel

        Setup guideYour gamertag, how consoles join, your servers, and a checklist of what's left. Import serversCopy servers in from Crafty Controller or another folder. Appearance and passwordLight or dark, change your password, sign out.

        Forgot your password?

        On the Unraid server, run this, then choose "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in page and type the code it prints. The code works once, for an hour.

        docker exec blockheads blockheads reset-password
